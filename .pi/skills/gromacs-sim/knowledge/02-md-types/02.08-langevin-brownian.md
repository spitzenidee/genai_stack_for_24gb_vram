# 02.08 — Langevin & Brownian Dynamics

**When to use**: couple the system to a **heat bath with friction** (Langevin /
stochastic dynamics) or simulate **over-damped** motion (Brownian dynamics) —
common for vacuum/sparse systems, enhanced equilibration of fast modes,
receptor–ligand docking-like sampling, or when hydrodynamic detail is
unwanted.

## Stochastic (velocity) Langevin dynamics
- Adds a **friction** and a **noise** term to Newton's equations:
  `m_i r̈_i = −m_i γ_i ṙ_i + F_i(r) + noise`, with noise variance
  `2 m_i γ_i k_B T δ(s)`.
- `1/γ_i` large vs. system timescales ≈ MD with stochastic temperature
  coupling; processes longer than `1/γ_i` (e.g. hydrodynamics) are damped.
- Each degree of freedom couples independently → **fast equilibration of fast
  modes**. Useful for **vacuum** systems.
- Integrator: **`sd`** (stochastic dynamics). **The old `langevin` and
  `brownian` integrator values are gone** (Brownian is `bd`; `cg` is
  conjugate-gradient energy minimization, **not** Langevin).

### Key MDP options
| Option | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `integrator` | `sd` (Langevin) or `bd` (Brownian). |
| `tau-t` | **Friction time constant (1/γ) for `sd`** — in 2026.3 this doubles as the friction control; **`tau-bfric` is gone**. |
| `ref-t` | Bath temperature. |
| `bd-fric` | Brownian friction coefficient (for `bd`). |
| `ld-seed` | Random seed for the noise. |
| `tc-grps` | **Required** — must cover **all** atoms (else `N atoms are not part of any of the T-Coupling groups`). |

**Note**: with `sd`/`bd`, the `tcoupl` *thermostat* is **ignored** — the
friction *is* the thermostat. **But** `tc-grps`, `ref-t`, and `tau-t` must
still be set (all with the **same number of entries**) or `grompp` aborts
with `Invalid T coupling input` / `N atoms are not part of any of the T-Coupling groups`.
For `sd`, `tau-t` is the friction (1/γ); for `bd`, `bd-fric` is the friction
and `tau-t` is present only to satisfy the size check (not used for friction).
`Andersen`/`Andersen-massive` are also available as `tcoupl` values for
`sd`/`bd` (set `nstcomm = 1`).

## Brownian dynamics (over-damped limit)
- High-friction limit of Langevin: inertia negligible.
  `ṙ_i = (F_i/γ_i) + noise`, integrated explicitly:
  `r_i(t+Δt) = r_i(t) + (Δt/γ_i) F_i + √(2 k_B T Δt/γ_i) r_i^G`.
- `γ_i` is the friction coefficient (amu/ps).
- Use when the system is genuinely over-damped (no inertial dynamics of
  interest).

## Choosing γ (friction)
- `γ` sets the bath correlation time `1/γ`. 
- Small `γ` → weak coupling (near-NMD with mild damping).
- Large `γ` → strong damping → approaches Brownian dynamics.
- Match `γ` to the physics: too large kills real dynamics; too small gives
  little benefit over plain MD.

## Typical workflow
1. Standard prep (`01.1–01.4`). Equilibration can be shorter (Langevin
   equilibrates fast modes quickly).
2. Set `integrator = sd`, `tau-t` (friction), `ref-t`, `tc-grps` (all atoms),
   `ld-seed` — or `integrator = bd`, `bd-fric`, `ref-t`, `tau-t`, `tc-grps`
   (all atoms), `ld-seed`.
3. Run; monitor temperature (bath) and the observable of interest.

## MDP deltas vs. standard
- `integrator = sd`/`bd`; `tau-t` (sd friction), `bd-fric` (bd), `ref-t`,
  `tc-grps` (all atoms — **required**), `ld-seed`.
- `tcoupl` is ignored (the friction *is* the thermostat), but `tc-grps` +
  `ref-t` + `tau-t` must all be present with matching counts.

## Analysis
- Standard (`01.8`), but interpret dynamics as **damped** — correlation times
  and diffusion are affected by `γ`. Report `γ` with any dynamic quantity.

## Pitfalls
- **Over-damping** real dynamics (γ too large) → wrong kinetics/diffusion.
- Forgetting Langevin is **not** a physical solvent model — it replaces
  explicit solvent friction with a Markovian bath (no hydrodynamics).
- `andersen` thermostat is a related but distinct velocity-randomization
  scheme (see `01.5`) — don't conflate.
- Reproducibility: set `ld-seed` for repeatable noise.
